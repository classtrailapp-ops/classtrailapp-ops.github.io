Classtrail

Classtrail is an independent app for university students: your timetable on a campus map, rooms, routes and "leave now" reminders. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by KTH Royal Institute of Technology or SL.

The room names and floor plans of KTH come from KTH Places (kth.se/places) and are shown from a copy that the app carries. If you represent KTH, or own any of the data that the app shows, and you want it removed, write to classtrail.app@gmail.com: the rooms and floor plans of KTH are turned off from a small file on this site (at the next start of the app), and anything else is removed in the next update of the app.
