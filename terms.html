1. The service, what is free and what is Premium

Classtrail is a campus map with a personal schedule. It is an independent app: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by KTH Royal Institute of Technology or SL.

Free, with no account and no payment: the map of the campuses (2D and 3D), the search of buildings, rooms and places, the floor plans, the routes (on foot, by bike, by e-bike, by e-scooter, accessible, indoors and outdoors), Nearby, parking, services, opening hours, the settings, the entry of your calendar link and the function to withdraw from the contract.

Premium (the plans of section 2): everything that reads your calendar and everything that alerts you: your classes on the map and the classes of the day, the day, week and month agenda, the sheets of your classes, extra calendars, your own events and activities, the notices when a class changes, the "leave now" alerts, the reminders, the summary of the morning, the check of the alerts and the widget of the next class. Without Premium you can still enter your calendar link: the app downloads and keeps your calendar on your phone and shows your next class on the screen that offers Premium, and nothing more of it, and it schedules no alert. What you entered or wrote (the link, extra calendars, your own events and activities, your settings) is kept when Premium ends and comes back when it starts again.

2. Plans, free trial and renewal of Premium

There are three ways to pay for Premium (the local price and the taxes are shown by the store before you buy):

  • Monthly subscription, 1.49 EUR a month. New subscribers get 7 days free. The subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in your Google Play or App Store account settings, at least 24 hours before the end of the period; cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Semester pass, 6.99 EUR once, for 6 months, and year pass, 9.99 EUR once, for 12 months. A pass is paid once and has no free trial. It does not renew by itself, and nothing more is charged when it ends: if you want to go on, you buy another pass. If you buy another one in the last 30 days of a pass, its months are added after the end of the first.

You can withdraw from the contract within 14 days of buying, in the app (Settings, "Withdraw from the contract here"); refunds are handled by the store under its own rules. Offers and coupons may change.

3. Accuracy

Timetables come from the link you provide. Public transport times come from SL and include its real-time estimate when SL provides one, but delays can change after the app has checked. Floor plans, room data and routes are approximate, indoor routes stay within one floor, and some doors are not marked on the plans. Free-computer figures are informational. Opening times of places come from OpenStreetMap and from the pages of the places themselves; they can be out of date or change without notice (holidays, summer, one-off closures), and the app shows where each comes from and how old it is. Do not rely on the app where a wrong time, route or room could cause harm; always check official sources.

Routes by bike, e-bike and e-scooter come from OpenStreetMap and are an aid, not a guarantee: nobody has ridden every way. Follow the traffic rules and the signs, ride only where it is allowed, get off where the app or a sign says so, and look at the road, not at the phone. The app does not know about roadworks, closures, ice or the weather.
