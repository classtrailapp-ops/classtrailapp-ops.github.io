Classtrail open map data

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The street map, the walking network, the bicycle network, the entrances, the places and the addresses of Classtrail are drawn and computed on the phone from data files that are derived from OpenStreetMap. This page offers those files to everyone, under the same licence, as the licence asks.

The 3D map

map3d.pmtiles is the archive of vector tiles (PMTiles version 3, Mapbox vector tiles, zoom 6 to 15) that the 3D map of the app draws: the ground (water, green, zones, roads, railways), every building with the height that OpenStreetMap gives (its height or building:levels, or a guess from its kind and size where there is none), the ribbons of glass that the app adds round tall towers, and the names of streets, places and buildings, for everything that the street maps of the app cover: the campuses, greater Stockholm and the ring round it. It is made from OpenStreetMap (and, for some footprints of the campuses, Overture Maps, under the same licence) by tiles3d.py with the tiler tiler.py; the README in the zip has the layers and the properties of each. What the app draws inside an opened building comes from KTH Places and is not part of this offer.

The buildings, the land use and the paths outside the campuses

vector_region_buildings.bin, vector_region_zones.bin and vector_region_paths.bin are the buildings (with the courtyards of the big ones), the land use (housing, shops and offices, industry, school and hospital grounds, car parks, pitches, pedestrian areas, platforms, sand and rock, wetland, railway land) and the paths (service roads and tracks, footways, paths, cycleways and steps, as open lines) of the whole area that the street map of the app covers, outside the boxes of the campuses, whose own street maps (vector_<campus>.json) have them. They are binary files made from OpenStreetMap (the extract of BBBike and, for the two strips of the area that it does not cover, the extract of Sweden of Geofabrik cut to the frame by cut_extract.py and chosen by strips.py) by build_region_buildings.py, build_region_zones.py and build_region_paths.py (the land use with the classes of add_map_zones.py, the paths with the levels of road_levels.py); the README in the zip has the format (region_pack.py, which also holds a reader and a writer).

The words of the map

map_labels.bin is the text of the street map of the app: the names of the streets (a label every 300 m of each named street), of the places (suburbs, neighbourhoods, villages, islands), of the lakes, bays and straits, of the parks, nature reserves and woods, of the stations and stops, of the shops, services and places of culture, education and health, and of the buildings that have a name, for the whole area that the street maps cover. Each label is a name as OpenStreetMap writes it (in the language of the place), a point, what it is, the zoom from which it is written and its priority. It is a binary file made from OpenStreetMap (the extract of BBBike; for the two strips of the area that it does not cover, the extract of Sweden of Geofabrik cut to the frame by cut_extract.py and chosen by strips.py; and, for the multipolygons that the extract of BBBike cannot assemble, the Overpass servers) by build_map_labels.py and fetch_label_extras.py with the rules of map_labels_osm.py; the README in the zip has the format (map_labels_pack.py, which also holds a reader and a writer).

The bicycle network

bike_graph.bin is the network the app uses to work out routes by bike, e-bike and e-scooter: the cycle ways, paths, footways, service roads and streets of greater Stockholm and of the surroundings of Södertälje and Haninge, as nodes and edges. Every edge says what kind of way it is (cycle way, shared path, street with a lane, quiet street, street, busy road, main road, or a stretch where the rider walks with the machine), its surface, whether it is one way for bicycles, and whether it is lit; some nodes are traffic lights, crossings or barriers. It is a binary file made from OpenStreetMap by build_bike_graph.py with the rules of bike_classify.py; the README in the zip has the format (bike_graph_codec.py, which also holds a reader and a writer) and the rules.

The places to park a bike

bike_parking.json lists the places where a bike, an e-bike or an e-scooter can be left near the campuses (bicycle parking and the charging stations for bikes of OpenStreetMap): the position, whether there is a roof, lockers or a charger, how many bikes fit, whether it is paid and whether it is for everybody. It was made by build_bike_parking.py, whose first lines describe the format; the places marked private in OpenStreetMap are only listed by their identifier, so that the app does not send anybody there.

The names of the buildings

building_labels_osm.json holds the names that OpenStreetMap gives to buildings and the street addresses (from the address points of OpenStreetMap) that label the others: text, position, rank (1 an OpenStreetMap name, 2 an address, 3 the nearest address), area and kind. It is the part of the file building_labels.json of the app that comes from OpenStreetMap; the 50 names that the app takes from KTH Places for its own buildings are not part of it and are not offered under this licence. It was made by build_building_labels.py, whose first lines describe the format.

The relief of the 3D map (not OpenStreetMap)

The shading of the hills that the 3D map draws at the zoom of the region comes from a different source: the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (a digital surface model of about 30 m), cut into tiles by relief.py into its own file, map3d_terrain.pmtiles. It is not made from OpenStreetMap, it is not under the ODbL and it is not part of the files offered on this page. What you may do

Copy, distribute and adapt these files under the ODbL: credit OpenStreetMap, say so if you changed them, and offer what you publish from them under the same licence. Some buildings were added from Overture Maps, which is also under the ODbL; its contributors include Esri Community Maps contributors (CC BY 4.0), Microsoft (ODbL) and Google (CC BY 4.0).

What is not included

The rooms, the floor plans and the names taken from KTH Places belong to KTH and are not part of this offer. In the files of gyms and scooter parking the field sources names where each kind of entry comes from: only the OpenStreetMap part is offered under the ODbL, the other sources keep their own terms.
