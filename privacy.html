Classtrail privacy policy

Version 6 · last updated 22 November 2026. Classtrail is an independent app for university students. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by KTH Royal Institute of Technology or SL.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of the personal data that reaches us (only the e-mails that you choose to send us, see section 3) is the developer of Classtrail: Ferran Saigi Zapata, Malvinas väg 18, lgh 11208, 114 28 Stockholm, Sweden. Contact: classtrail.app@gmail.com.

2. Short version

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server of ours. Your timetable, settings, home point and activities are kept on your phone. We do not receive or store any of them. To do its job the app sends some requests straight from your phone to the services listed below, and some of them go out without you doing anything at that moment: your private timetable link goes to the site that hosts your calendar every time the app downloads it, also in the background (Android); and, only if you chose the Transit way to get to class and set a home point, your home point and the times of your classes go to SL's journey planner (table below). The street map makes no requests at all; the optional Google layers of the map (the Google street map, the Google hybrid and the Google satellite; off until you switch one on) are made by Google's Maps SDK and do contact Google, see below.

Google map layers (Google Maps SDK). Only while you use a layer drawn by Google (the Google street map, the Google hybrid or the Google satellite), Google's Maps SDK, inside the app, asks Google for the map of the area you are looking at and Google receives, according to its documentation of the SDK: your phone's IP address, the model and system version of the phone and the version of the SDK, crash reports of the SDK, a pseudonymous identifier of the SDK (to count daily users), and which part of the map you move and zoom to. On an iPhone the SDK declares the same kinds of data to Apple: crash data, performance data, a device identifier, a user identifier of the SDK and how the map is used. If you allowed location, the blue dot that shows where you are on that map is drawn by the SDK on your phone. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own privacy policy; we receive none of it. The app does not give Google your timetable, your home point or your activities. While a Google layer is chosen, the app also opens a connection to Google's map server about every 20 seconds (every 4 seconds while it does not answer) to learn whether Google can be reached, and closes it at once without sending anything (Google sees the IP address, as with the map itself); it does this only while the app is in front, and when Google cannot be reached the app shows its own map until it can. If you do not use a Google layer, nothing is sent to Google by the map. The legal notices of the SDK are in the app (Settings, About).

Every web request exposes your phone's IP address to the site that receives it, as with any app. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The app does not request background location. It does download your calendar (and, with Transit, ask SL) in the background, as described in the rows above.

3b. Who receives data, and international transfers

  • The site that hosts your calendar (the operator of the address you paste: for example KTH, Stockholm University, TimeEdit or Daisy): your private link and your IP address. You choose this site by pasting its link; its own privacy policy applies.
  • AB Storstockholms Lokaltrafik (SL), Sweden (sl.se): the journey planner receives what the row on the home point says, only with the Transit way.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts the pages of this project (this policy, the terms, the status file and the page of a shared room): it sees the IP address, the time and the address requested. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google LLC (United States): the Google Play store and billing, and, only if you use a Google layer of the map in a build that has it, the Maps SDK (see above). Google privacy policy. Apple Inc. for purchases in the App Store: Apple privacy policy.
  • KTH (Kungliga Tekniska högskolan, kth.se): the public room page of the computer room you look at.

GitHub and Google are based in the United States, outside the European Economic Area. The transfer is made by those companies under their own safeguards (for example the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses); we do not send them anything ourselves. The e-mails you send us are kept in a Google mailbox.

4. Legal basis and retention

Processing happens on your device or at your request (performance of the contract with you, and your consent when you allow location or use a Google layer of the map; you can withdraw the consent at any time in the settings of your phone, without affecting what was done before). We keep nothing on our side, except what you choose to send us by e-mail (a message to the support address, a report with a position, a statement of withdrawal): a report with a position is kept until the correction has been checked and made and deleted within 12 months at the latest; other messages are kept as long as they are needed to answer you; a statement of withdrawal is kept to make the refund and to be able to show that it was made. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. Backups: on Android the app tells the system to leave its data out of the cloud backups and out of the transfer to a new phone, so what it stores does not travel; on an iPhone, if you back the phone up with iCloud or with a computer, what the app stored on the phone, your calendar link included, is part of that backup, under Apple’s terms (the app neither makes nor reads the backup). The copy as text that you can ask for in the app (section 3) is yours to keep or not.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Apart from the e-mails that you send us (write to the address above and we will answer, correct or delete them), we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing else for us to access, correct, move or delete. You can delete everything on your phone with the button "Delete everything" of the screen "What this phone keeps" (Settings), which lists exactly what the app stores, by clearing the app's storage or by uninstalling it. You may contact us at the address above, and you may complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work (in Sweden: IMY, imy.se).

6. Children

The app is meant for university students and is not directed at children.
